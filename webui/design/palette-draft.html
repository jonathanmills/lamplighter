Where Carolina Blue goes

Carolina Blue is the interface's main accent and its main data color. It is never used for large flat areas. When it fills a background, it's a soft tint.

Charts: one series

Bars and histograms with one series use Carolina Blue on dark backgrounds (passes, 4.5:1 against the panel) and Carolina Deep in the light theme, where Carolina Blue itself is too pale (2.5:1).

Charts: ordered buckets (data age)

Age bands keep a natural order, so they take a single-hue blue ramp rather than mixed colors: pale for new data, deep for cold data. The ramp passes the ordinal checks in both themes.

Soft backgrounds

A 12–20% tint of Carolina Blue marks selected rows, hover states and information notes. The tint is see-through, so it lies on charcoal or paper without becoming a bright block.

The lightest color in the light theme is the surface #ECE9E4, a warm paper gray. There is no pure white anywhere: the light theme is meant to be calm, not glaring.
